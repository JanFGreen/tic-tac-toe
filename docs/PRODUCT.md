# Product

A tic-tac-toe game in the browser that is a pleasure to play on a phone and on a desktop.

- Players: two people on one device, or one person against the computer.
- The computer has difficulty levels, from beginner-friendly to unbeatable.
- The game is clear at a glance: whose turn it is, who won and with which line, a draw.
- Scores of the current session are visible; a new round starts in one action.
- Playable with mouse, touch and keyboard only; readable for screen readers.
- Later: results against the computer go to a small server so players see a leaderboard.

Language of the UI: English.
